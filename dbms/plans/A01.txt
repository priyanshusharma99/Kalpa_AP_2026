Query ID: A01
Type: READ
Description: Spatial Viewport Culling (2D Bounding Box Intersection)

SQL:
SELECT e.element_id, e.shape_type, e.pos_x, e.pos_y, e.width, e.height, e.z_index
FROM canvas_elements e
WHERE e.project_id = 'proj_001'
  AND e.pos_x <= 1920.00
  AND (e.pos_x + e.width) >= 0.00
  AND e.pos_y <= 1080.00
  AND (e.pos_y + e.height) >= 0.00
ORDER BY e.z_index ASC;
